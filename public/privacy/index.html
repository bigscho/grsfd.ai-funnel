Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

GrassFed Services LLC ("GrassFed," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard personal information in connection with our email marketing services (the "Services").

This Privacy Policy applies to: - Agents and Clients — real estate professionals who use the Services. - Recipients — homeowners and other individuals whose contact information is used in Campaigns sent through the Services.

This Privacy Policy is incorporated by reference into the Terms of Service. Agents are also subject to Section 19 (Data Processing Terms) of the Terms of Service, which governs the Agent's relationship with GrassFed as it relates to Recipient data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Agents provide

When you subscribe to the Services, we collect: - Name and contact information (email, phone number, business address) - Business information (brokerage legal name, DBA, real estate license number, license type, license state, supervising broker) - Billing information (processed securely through Stripe) - Territory preferences, MLS identifier, MLS event filters, and Campaign criteria - Custom email copy and outreach persona configuration - Block lists of contacts to exclude

1.2 Information we collect automatically from Agents

When you use the Services, we automatically collect: - Campaign performance data (sends, opens, replies, bounces) - Login and access timestamps - Device and browser information - IP address - Subject lines, headers, send timestamps, and opt-out events maintained as a compliance log (see Section 4)

1.3 Recipient data sourced from third parties

We obtain homeowner contact data from third-party data providers to power Campaigns sent on behalf of Agents. This data may include: - Names and email addresses - Phone numbers (where available) - Property addresses and property characteristics - Other publicly-available real estate data

GrassFed sources Recipient data from third-party providers and may, depending on the activities involved, qualify as a "data broker" under California Civil Code § 1798.99.80 et seq., Vermont 9 V.S.A. § 2446, Texas Bus. & Com. Code Ch. 509, and Oregon HB 2052. GrassFed has registered as a data broker in jurisdictions where required. See Section 8 for Recipient rights and the data-broker delete-request process.

Recipient data sourced for an Agent's Campaigns is made available to that Agent through the Services for the purpose of operating those Campaigns and managing the resulting replies and opt-outs.

1.4 Categories of personal information (CCPA / CPRA enumeration)

For purposes of California Civil Code § 1798.140 and equivalent state privacy laws, the categories of personal information we collect are: - Identifiers — name, email, phone number, mailing address, IP address, account identifiers - Commercial information — billing records, transaction history, subscription details - Internet or other electronic network activity — login timestamps, device and browser information - Geolocation data — coarse, derived from IP address; mailing address for billing - Professional or employment-related information (Agents only) — license number, license type, brokerage affiliation, DBA registration - Inferences — derived from the above to operate and improve the Services

We do not collect biometric information, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, union membership, or other "sensitive personal information" categories as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to: - Provide and operate the Services - Process payments and manage subscriptions - Send Campaigns on behalf of Agents - Receive, route, and process replies, opt-outs, and bounces - Communicate with Agents about accounts and Campaigns - Provide customer support - Maintain a global suppression list across all Agents - Improve the Services and develop new features - Comply with legal obligations, including subpoenas, regulatory inquiries, and data-broker delete requests - Protect against fraud and abuse

2.1 Master inbox; staff and AI access to replies

Replies, opt-outs, and bounces from Recipients are routed into GrassFed's master inbox and the Agent's portal. GrassFed personnel and automated systems (including machine-learning systems used for sentiment analysis, opt-out detection, and deliverability anomaly flagging) may access the contents of replies, opt-outs, and bounces in the course of operating the Services, providing support, ensuring quality, and processing opt-outs. GrassFed does not use AI to generate or send Campaign content without human review, and does not use Recipient reply contents to train models served to third parties.

8. Email Campaign Recipients

If you received an email from one of our Agents' Campaigns:

  • How we got your information. Your contact information was obtained from public records or third-party data providers (see Section 1.3). The real estate Agent on whose behalf the email was sent is identified in the email's body and signature.
  • How to opt out. You can opt out by replying to the email with "stop," "remove," "unsubscribe," or a similar instruction. Opt-out requests are processed automatically and added to our permanent global suppression list, which applies across all Agents on the GrassFed platform. Once you opt out, you will not receive further Campaigns from any GrassFed Agent.
  • About this email and GrassFed's role. The email was transmitted from GrassFed-owned sending infrastructure on behalf of the identified Agent. GrassFed personnel and automated systems may access the contents of any reply you send to a Campaign email, including for purposes of processing your opt-out, providing support to the Agent, ensuring quality, and detecting deliverability issues (see Section 2.1).
  • Questions about a specific email should be directed to the real estate Agent identified in the message.
  • Data broker delete requests. GrassFed is registered as a data broker in jurisdictions where required (see Section 1.3). California residents may submit deletion requests through the California Privacy Protection Agency's data broker registry. Residents of any jurisdiction may also submit a delete request directly to GrassFed by emailing admin@grassfed.tech with "Data Broker Delete Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days and will, where the request is verified, delete the information across our systems and add the email address to our permanent suppression list to prevent future contact.
