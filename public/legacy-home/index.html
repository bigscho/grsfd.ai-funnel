Same homeowner data underneath — very different ways to reach it.

grsfd

Event-triggered, surgical.

  1. 1

    We detect a listing or closing.

    Anytime something goes pending, active, or sold in your MLS, grsfd sees it the same day.

  2. 2

    We search the nearest neighbors.

    grsfd pulls every homeowner within your chosen radius of that property — then verifies their emails.

  3. 3

    We deploy the campaign.

Territory-wide, at scale.

  1. 1

    Zip codes, counties, or cities — plus simple filters like minimum estimated value or last-sale date.

  2. 2

    We aggregate the homeowners.

  3. 3

    We run one large-scale campaign.

    A single cold-email sequence goes out to the full list, warmed and compliant, replies routed back to you.
