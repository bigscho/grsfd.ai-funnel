03 / 12 Avoiding spam
Homeowner, You know how you can use a blast software like MailChimp/ConstantContact/etc. to email clients? A lot of agents are curious why they can't just cold email with those types of tools. Those tools are really good for what they're designed for: 1. Newsletters 2. Drip campaigns 3. Promos BUT only to your past clients. These people engage, and are way less likely to opt-out. If we blast a cold list of strangers, we will go to spam. Plain and simple. Grassfed's sending platform is different in a couple ways: 1. No blasting: We randomize sending and spread it out across days instead of minutes... 2. Rotating sender accounts: Never override a singular sender account This is how we respect Google's sending guidelines and spam restrictions at a large scale.

07 / 12 Another secret
Homeowner, You know how emails will bounce sometimes? That's due to an invalid email address. Now imagine we're going to email a whole town or city for you. There will be a lot of invalid emails that we don't want to touch... A HUGE missed step in B2C cold email is email validation. Before we send, we need to have the cleanest list possible of active emails and get rid of any inactive emails. We call this "List Hygiene" and we do this before we ever move contacts into your account. Low bounce rates = high response rates...
